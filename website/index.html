Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about mobile nail service in Butte County.

Does Olya's Mobile Nails come to my home in Chico?

Yes! We travel directly to your home, office, or event anywhere in Butte County including Chico, Paradise, Magalia, Oroville, and Gridley. No need to leave your house — we bring the salon to you.

What mobile nail services do you offer?

We offer three signature pedicures (Essential Mobile Pedicure, Comfort & Renew, and the Restore & Relax Signature Pedicure), a Structured Gel Manicure, and Gel-X Full Set extensions — all performed at your location in Butte County.

How do I book a mobile nail appointment?

Use the booking form below, or call/text us at (530) 588-3315. We typically confirm within a few hours. For urgent requests, texting is fastest!

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking 2–3 days in advance for your preferred time, though same-day appointments are sometimes available — just call or text us to check availability.

Is there a travel fee for mobile nail services?

Travel within Chico is included in service pricing. A small travel fee may apply for Paradise, Magalia, Oroville, or Gridley — we'll always be upfront about this at the time of booking.

Are your products safe and professional-grade?

Absolutely. We use only salon-quality, professional-grade products. All tools are sanitized between every client using hospital-grade disinfectant. Your health and safety is our top priority.

Do you do group events like bridal parties or birthdays?

Yes! We love doing group events. Contact us for custom pricing on bridal parties, bachelorettes, birthdays, and corporate events across Butte County.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept cash, Venmo, CashApp, and Zelle. Contact us if you have questions about payment options.
