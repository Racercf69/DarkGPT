What We Do

Cleanout Services in Butte County

Whether it's a single room or an entire property, we adapt to what you need.

Estate & Family Cleanouts

Clearing a loved one's home is overwhelming. We work with care and discretion — sorting through belongings respectfully, setting aside items for family, donating, and hauling. We're not in a rush.

Garage, Shed & Storage Cleanouts

FAQ

Common Questions

Still have questions? Text us directly — we respond the same day.

How do photo estimates work?

Text a few photos of the space or items to (530) 518-1198. We'll review them and send back a written estimate — usually within a few hours during business hours. For larger jobs we may follow up with a quick call or a free on-site look.

Do you handle estate cleanouts?

Yes — it's one of the most common things we do. We work alongside families clearing a loved one's home with care and discretion. We sort through belongings, set aside items the family wants to keep, donate what we can to local organizations, and haul the rest. We're patient and not in a rush.

Do you donate usable items?

Absolutely. We take a donation-first approach — furniture, clothing, kitchenware, books, and household goods in decent condition go to local Butte County thrift stores and nonprofits. We document what we donate and can share receipts if needed for tax purposes.

Do you haul everything away?

Yes. We haul furniture, appliances, tools, boxes, yard debris, and general junk. We sort, load, donate what's usable, and properly dispose of the rest. You don't need to do any lifting or hauling — that's what we're there for.

What areas do you serve?

We serve all of Butte County — Chico, Paradise, Magalia, Oroville, Durham, Gridley, and surrounding communities. If you're not sure whether we reach your location, text us your address and we'll confirm quickly.

How much does a cleanout cost?

Pricing starts around $75 for a single-item pickup and ranges from $250–$300+ for garage and move-out cleanouts. Estate cleanouts are quoted by the job. Final pricing depends on volume of items, labor time, access (stairs, tight spaces), disposal fees, and how much can be donated. Text photos and we'll give you a real number.
